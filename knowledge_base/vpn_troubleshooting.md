## VPN Troubleshooting
1. Check internet connection before attempting VPN
2. Update VPN client, outdated ones fail in silence
3. Disconnect, wait about 30 seconds, then reconnect
4. Restart VPN client entirely

### Escalation triggers
- Cert errors on connect
- All users at a location are affected
- Issue persists after full client reinstall
